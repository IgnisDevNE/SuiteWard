All recorded transitions were independently replayed. Replay establishes the behavioral difference and ancestry; it cannot independently establish the timing of the original observation. The machine-readable record retains that caveat.

I's composed tests verify already implemented adapter behavior rather than inventing a new implementation cycle. They use actual PostgreSQL and filesystem objects to prove missing/corrupt content rejection, complete rollback after a real SQL failure, independently published orphan content and safe retry, two-read-boundary promotion concurrency, and original historical v1 bytes distinct from healthy current v2.
