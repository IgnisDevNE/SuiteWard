# M1.7c: Lower latency

**Goal:** cut the time from a PR event (a command, a push or a merge) to its check or comment to well under a minute. The M1.7b live run measured about 2 to 3 minutes from a command to its answer. The owner approved this on 2026-10-15 (D-SYNC amendment).

**Depends on:** M1.7b.

**Owner answers (2026-10-16):**
- **Watermark:** the cycle start in GitHub's clock (`Date`), with a 10 s margin.
- **Wake-ups:** after the commit, with the periodic runs as the fallback.
- **Orbit:** the owner starts the node; tasks go to it while the owner is present.

**Contracts:**
- [runtime](../../contracts/runtime.md#latency-m17c-normative), "Latency (M1.7c)";
- [persistence](../../contracts/persistence.md#cycle-start-m17c-normative), "Cycle start (M1.7c)";
- the D-SYNC and D-COMMANDS amendments of 2026-10-16 in [open decisions](../decisions.md).

**Exit:**
- **Watermark.** A command waits for a cycle that started, in GitHub's clock, at least 10 s after it. `JOB_TIMEOUT` no longer enters the horizon.
- **Sync.** A tick every 20 s, paced to stay at or under 450 requests a minute.
- **Wake-ups.** Sync wakes commands, checks and merges; commands wake checks and bootstrap; a promotion wakes checks.
- **Live targets, both targets:**
  - a command answered in under 45 s;
  - an approval reflected in the check in under 60 s;
  - a push reflected in the check in under 45 s.

**Where the time went** (`main` `eba8db3`):
- The commands horizon was the earliest `LastSuccess` minus `JOB_TIMEOUT` minus 30 s, about 90 s.
- `LastSuccess` is the local clock at the end of the listing.
- The sync ran every 60 s.
- Nothing was chained.
