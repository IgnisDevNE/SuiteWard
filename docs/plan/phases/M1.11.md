# M1.11: Close-out and robustness

**Goal:** close what SH left and harden the runtime before the final `v0.2.0`: the pace and the status of the poll, the bounded caches, the retired Deploy workflow, and the process rules learned this week. The phase ships as `v0.2.0-rc.3`.

**Decision:** owner, 2026-10-21. One phase joins the close-out and the robustness work.

**Rules:**
- **Test first** for every behavior change ([TDD rule](../../tdd.md)).
- **The repository is public.** Findings are referred to by id, never described.
- **This repository is governed by SuiteWard.** Protected files change in as few pushes as possible.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-21)

Recorded in the [decision register](../decisions.md).

1. **One phase, M1.11, ships as `v0.2.0-rc.3`.** The final `v0.2.0` comes after it, cut from a revision validated on A3 (co-located), the AlmaLinux 9 host (isolated) and a throwaway AlmaLinux VM that runs [install 11.5](../../install.md#11-updating) live: the in-place upgrade from v0.1.3 to rc.3.
2. **The Deploy workflow is retired** (D-DEPLOY): `deploy.yml`, the `:deploy` tag and the `remote-poc` environment. CI builds the image without pushing it. Releases are the only published images.
3. **D-JOB-RETENTION:** River's defaults, with no configuration: completed jobs 24 hours, cancelled and discarded jobs 7 days.

## Dropped and deferred

Dropped after the code inventory, with the reason:
- the wrong-code wording: already correct;
- more than 100 open pull requests: the sync already paginates;
- `AppendProposalRevision`: already validates the project, suite, carrier and origin;
- the "M2" lint debt: that was M1.10's task M2, and no exclusion remains;
- deterministic blob-read errors: the first failure cancels the rest, and the order was accepted in PR #44;
- the blob cache clear-all: a documented shortcut;
- the wake-up race: documented, costs one extra run;
- confusable characters: a product policy, not a defect.

Deferred: a merge before V1 whose tree stays unreadable stalls the repository's queue. It goes to the scope-discovery and bootstrap work.
