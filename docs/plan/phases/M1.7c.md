# M1.7c: Lower latency

**Goal:** cut the time from a PR event (a command, a push or a merge) to its check or comment from 20 s–2 min, as measured in M1.7, to well under a minute. The owner approved this on 2026-10-15 (D-SYNC amendment).

**Depends on:** M1.7b.

**Decision first.** The D-COMMANDS watermark (the earliest synchronization success minus `JOB_TIMEOUT` minus 30 s) keeps every command waiting about 90 s, so chaining jobs alone cannot beat it. Decide a horizon that keeps the ordering guarantee and is measured closer to the cycle, for example from the cycle's start.

**Planned work:**
- **Chained jobs:** each enqueue runs in the same transaction, as a unique insert that coalesces with the periodic run.
  - A processed approve or revoke enqueues `github_checks` (and `github_bootstrap` for a baseline).
  - A promotion enqueues `github_checks`.
  - A synchronization that sees new comments enqueues `github_commands`.
- **A shorter synchronization poll,** about 20–30 s.

Webhooks come after the MVP. Refined when M1.7b is merged.
